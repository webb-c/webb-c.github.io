How this site works

The concept of these puzzles is highly motivated by Baba Is You. All the basic ideas come from that game, and if you have not played it yet, I recommend giving this fun game a try.

Every rule is a sentence made of word blocks, such as webbisyou or webbreadpaper. Push the blocks and the sentence changes, and with it, what is true. Reaching something that is win clears a stage; reading something opens it.

You can still see all the information on this homepage without the puzzles.

Rather just read? “Hide puzzles” under any board hides them on every page; the menu at the top goes to the same places.
